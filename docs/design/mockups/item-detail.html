Item detail, v3

One filled button, one status chip, one sheet over the photo. The bottom nav steps aside so the action bar is the only thing at the bottom. Light theme only; tokens are the app's own.

What changed from the screenshot

  • Sheet over the photo. The photo is near-square (380dp on a 360dp-wide phone, down from about 45% of the screen) and the details sheet rises 24dp over it, so price and seller show without scrolling.
  • One status chip, only when it matters. "Available" is the normal state, so it is no longer shown. Gold chip for "Only N left", grey for "On hold" or "Sold out". Crimson goes back to meaning price and the main action.
  • No bottom nav on this screen. The action bar becomes the only bottom element. Back returns to the tab you came from with it intact.
  • Contact is quiet. Call and WhatsApp are white tonal buttons inside the seller card. WhatsApp keeps its green on the icon only. Follow becomes a small pill on the seller row. The only filled button on the screen is Buy now.
  • Collection is a light row above the seller card, not stacked inside it.
  • Pager dots replace "3 / 3" and sit in a small blurred capsule so they read on any photo.

Motion and feel (Apple principles)

  • Parallax. The photo scrolls at half speed under the sheet. The top buttons stay put and fade to an ivory app bar with the title once the sheet reaches the top.
  • Press feedback on touch-down. Buttons scale to 0.97 with a spring (damping 1.0, about 0.3s). Heart uses a short bounce because the tap carries intent.
  • Spatial consistency. Photo grows from the catalog tile and returns to it on back (already wired via sharedPhoto).
  • Materials. Round buttons and the action bar are translucent over content; never one light blur on another.
  • Type. Title 22 / price 32 in Bricolage with tight tracking; body in Work Sans at 14 to 16. Three sizes carry the page.

Not in this mockup, on purpose

  • Spec grid (occasion, length, blouse piece). The API only returns type, fabric, colour and upload date today, so there is nothing real to show. Colour appears on the meta line when set.
  • Wholesale features from the Stitch v2 reference stay out of scope.
  • Dark theme.
